Examined vulnerabilities in Vision Transformers (ViTs), revealing how small input changes affect layer-wise representations. Proposed NeuroShield-ViT to neutralize vulnerable neurons and enhance adversarial robustness. Improved classification accuracy by 54.85% against PGD attacks and 71.6% against IGO attacks on ImageNet1K.
