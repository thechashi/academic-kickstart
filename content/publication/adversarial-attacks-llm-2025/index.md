abstract: |
  As Large Language Models (LLMs) have become integral to numerous practical applications, ensuring their robustness and safety is critical. LLMs remain susceptible to adversarial inputs designed to exploit vulnerabilities. Existing adversarial attack methods have notable limitations: discrete token-based methods suffer from inefficiency, whereas continuous optimization methods typically fail to produce valid tokens from the model's vocabulary. This paper proposes a novel technique for adversarial attacks that overcomes these limitations by leveraging regularized gradients with continuous optimization methods. The approach achieves a two-order-of-magnitude speed improvement compared to the state-of-the-art greedy coordinate gradient-based method, substantially boosting the attack success rate on aligned language models.
