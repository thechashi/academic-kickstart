abstract: |
  Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are often susceptible to hallucinations, where they generate incorrect or nonsensical content based on visual inputs. In this poster, we examine the role of vision embeddings in triggering these hallucination vulnerabilities. By analyzing the embedding space and its interaction with the language model component, we identify specific patterns and representation gaps that lead to misinterpretations and hallucinated outputs. Our findings provide insights into improving the reliability of MLLMs by addressing these embedding-level vulnerabilities.
