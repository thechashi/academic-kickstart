abstract: |
  We introduce SpatialViLT, a vision-language model designed to improve spatial grounding and cross-modal alignment in multimodal intelligence systems. By integrating depth maps, 3D coordinates, and edge representations through a multi-task learning framework, SpatialViLT enhances 3D scene understanding for robotic navigation and manipulation tasks. Our experiments on the Visual Spatial Reasoning (VSR) dataset demonstrate that our approach improves spatial reasoning benchmark performance by 2.1%.
